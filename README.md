🏏 PSL 2026 Auction — Excel Analytics Dashboard

A structured, data-rich Excel dashboard analyzing the complete **HBL PSL 2026 Player Auction**, covering all 104 auctioned players, retained squads, direct signings, team budgets, and key analytics insights.

🚀 How to Use
> Download the `.xlsx` file and open in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets

---

## 📊 What's Inside (4 Sheets)

### 1. 📋 Auction Data
- All **104 successfully auctioned players**
- Columns: Player, Country, Team, Base Price, Sale Price, Price Jump, Multiplier, Type (Local/Overseas)
- Color-coded by franchise colors
- Freeze panes for easy scrolling

### 2. 📌 Retained & Direct Signings
- All **retained players** per team with retention categories (Platinum, Diamond, Gold, Silver, Emerging)
- All **direct overseas signings** (Steve Smith PKR 14cr, Moeen Ali, Devon Conway, Marnus Labuschagne, Mustafizur Rahman)

### 3. 💰 Team Budgets
- Each team's **base purse: PKR 45 Crore**
- Breakdown: Auction Spend + Retention Spend + Direct Signings
- **Remaining purse** calculated with live formulas
- Bar chart: Total spend comparison across all 8 teams

### 4. 📈 Analytics
- **Top 10 most expensive players** (gold/silver/bronze highlights)
- **Top 10 biggest bidding wars** — players whose price jumped most from base
- **Country breakdown** — players sold and total spend by nationality
- **Local vs Overseas** spend breakdown with pie chart
